Troostwijk blocks automated visitors, so the bot doesn't scan it. These links open Troostwijk's own search for your watchlist in the Netherlands. Look for "faillissement" or "bedrijfsbeëindiging" in the auction name. Tip: save a search on Troostwijk to get their email alerts.

How the numbers work

You pay = bid + buyer's premium, plus VAT on both, plus any fixed fee per lot.

Resale = Marktplaats median asking price × your sell percentage. Click “listings” to see the Marktplaats listings it's based on. Asking prices are not sold prices.

Margin = resale − what you pay, and as a % of what you pay.

Bids are from the morning scan. Always check the live bid, the lot description, pickup date and the auction terms before bidding.
